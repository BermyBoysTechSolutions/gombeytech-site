Cloud inference

Cloud and SER5 tiers

Chat prompts go to the customer-selected AI provider, and document text also goes to the separately selected embeddings provider for search. The customer owns and pays both provider accounts; their terms govern those requests. Hosting the workspace on a VPS or appliance does not make cloud inference local.

Local inference

Local AI tier

For the agreed workflow, a model runs on the customer’s workstation without sending prompts to a third-party model API. Connected services, off-device backups, or remote support may still transmit data and are reviewed separately.

Scope and access
