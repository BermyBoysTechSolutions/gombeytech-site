Cloud inference

Cloud and SER5 tiers

The workspace is hosted on a dedicated VPS or the customer’s on-site SER5, but prompts still go to the selected cloud AI provider. The customer owns that provider account and pays its usage charges directly; provider terms govern its handling of prompts.

Local inference

Local AI tier

For the agreed workflow, a model runs on the customer’s workstation without sending prompts to a third-party model API. Connected services, off-device backups, or remote support may still transmit data and are reviewed separately.

Scope and access

Configured boundaries

Each customer gets a separately configured deployment with named accounts and the roles included in its tier. Exact file access, provider settings, integrations, backup path, and acceptance checks are written into the deployment scope.

How it works
